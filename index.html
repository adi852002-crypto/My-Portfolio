About

The unglamorous part is the part that matters.

I've spent most of my career inside operations that can't afford to go down — enterprise telecom support, service delivery for global accounts, and more recently the business side of transformation projects.

Nine of those years were at Tata Consultancy Services in Mumbai. First on the Telstra Australia account, running quality, coaching and SLA governance across roughly 200 associates serving enterprise customers. Then leading specialist escalation operations for Ultimatix, working across HR, IT, procurement and SAP Ariba, and building the reporting that leadership actually acted on. Across both accounts I also carried customer success: account health, retention conversations, and being the person clients escalated to when something had gone wrong.

In early 2026 I joined Dynamic Travels as Chief Business Officer. I owned a CRM implementation end to end from the business side — requirements, workflows, priorities, UAT — alongside a website rebuild and a full set of SOPs across sales, operations, marketing and support, and remain on-call as a strategic advisor to the leadership team today.

What I care about is whether a process still holds when the person who wrote it leaves. So I write documentation people will actually open, coach the team that has to live with the change, and keep the numbers honest enough to make decisions from.

For businesses

Three engagements, in more than one line.

The track record below is the full history. These three are the ones worth reading in detail — what was broken, what I did about it, and what was true when I left.

Business transformation

Dynamic Travels — CRM and operations, built from zero

 Chief Business Officer & Business Transformation Consultant · 2026
The problem

No formal CRM, no SOPs across sales, operations, marketing or support, and a website that no longer reflected the business. Decisions were being made without a shared source of truth.

What I did

Acted as business owner for a CRM implementation — requirements, workflows, feature priorities and UAT — while coordinating an external technology team. Redesigned the customer journey and directed a website modernization and digital roadmap. Wrote SOPs across all four functions and introduced AI-assisted workflows for reporting and documentation.

Where it landed

CRM live and in use, SOPs in place across the business, and a retained relationship: I stayed on as on-call strategic advisor to leadership after the core engagement closed — still active today.

Escalation governance

TCS — Ultimatix, the backbone leadership actually trusted

 Project Lead / Process Lead · Oct 2019 – Jan 2026
The problem

Enterprise escalations spanning HR, IT, SAP Ariba and Procurement had no single owner. Reporting existed, but leadership didn't plan around it, and onboarding and access governance were inconsistent across functions.

What I did

Took ownership of specialist enterprise escalation operations and became the standing liaison across HR, IT, SAP Ariba and Procurement. Formalised onboarding and access-management governance, and built the executive MIS dashboards and RCA documentation the business used to make decisions.

Where it landed

Six years running the function with sustained cross-functional trust, and a reporting cadence formal enough that leadership planned around it rather than asking for it.

Service delivery at scale

TCS — Telstra Australia, quality across ~200 associates

 Process Lead / Team Lead / Trainer · Aug 2016 – Oct 2019
The problem

An enterprise account at real scale — roughly 200 associates — needed consistent SLA performance, a bench that stayed trained, and a steady hand on the escalations that reached leadership.

What I did

Owned SLA adherence, quality and coaching across the account. Ran new-hire training and mentoring to keep the bench ready, and handled critical customer escalations directly with cross-functional stakeholders.

Where it landed

Three years of sustained operational governance at scale — the track record that moved directly into the standing escalation leadership role at Ultimatix next.
